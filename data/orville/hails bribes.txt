#
# These wire up the four hail channels that hails.txt leaves unused, so that a
# faction still speaks in character when you try to buy it off or when you have
# already knocked its systems out. Semantics verified in Government.cpp:207-234
# and 505-521.
#
# Rejection-only (no acceptance phrase) is the stock idiom for "this faction
# cannot be bribed" -- data/governments.txt:2159 does exactly that for the
# Republic. The Union, the Krill, the Calivon and the Kaylon are all modelled
# that way, for very different reasons.
