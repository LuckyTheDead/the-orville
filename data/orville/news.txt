# --- Union politics and bureaucracy -----------------------------------------
# The Union's defining texture: an enormous, well-meaning, deeply procedural
# institution that solves problems with committees and then apologizes for them.

news "unn union bureaucracy"
	location
		government "Republic"
	name
		phrase
			"union anchors"
	message
		word
			`"`
		word
			"The Union Council has concluded its third week of debate on the Rimward Expansion Bill. No vote was scheduled. A spokesperson described the session as 'productive,' a word Union watchers have learned to translate as 'nothing happened.'"
			"A procedural objection from the Science Directorate has delayed the appointment of a new Admiral of the Fleet for the fourth consecutive month. In the absence of a confirmed admiral, the fleet is being administered by a rotating committee of captains. Morale reports are described as 'mixed.'"
			"The Union Interior Ministry reminds all captains that planetary landing permits must be renewed before the fiscal quarter ends. Permits submitted after the deadline will be processed in the following quarter. The Ministry thanks you for your patience."
			"Union legislators have voted to fund a study on the feasibility of a study regarding faster-than-light communication relay placement. The study is expected to recommend further study."
			"An audit of the Union's exploratory fleet found that 61 percent of surveyed systems had already been charted by other species, 22 percent had been charted twice by the Union itself, and 3 percent had been assigned two different names by two different directorates. 'This is normal,' the audit concluded."
			"The Union has announced a new initiative to improve the welfare of deep-space crews. It consists of a pamphlet. The pamphlet is available on request at any Union spaceport."
			"Union Command has denied reports that it maintains a fleet of unregistered vessels for sensitive operations. The denial was issued by an unregistered vessel."
			"A Union ethics panel has ruled that captains may not use their authority to resolve personal disputes. The ruling was issued after a captain resolved a personal dispute using his authority."
			"The Union Council has passed a resolution affirming the Union's commitment to peace, cooperation, and the peaceful resolution of all disputes. The resolution passed unanimously after the dissenting members were reassigned."
			"Union spacers are reminded that hailing an unfamiliar vessel is a courtesy, not a request for permission. Failure to respond to a hail is legal. Failure to respond to a second hail is considered rude."
		word
			`"`
