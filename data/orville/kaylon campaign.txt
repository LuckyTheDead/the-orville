# The Orville: The Kaylon Arc.
#
# A six-chapter campaign covering first contact with the Kaylon, the defection
# of Isaac, the Kaylon advance on Union space, the coalition war, and the
# resolution. Missions chain through the "kaylon arc: *" conditions; each
# chapter ends with a condition that unlocks the next.
#
# Design notes:
#   * Every mission is `landing`, so it is offered at a spaceport.
#   * Conversations use explicit `goto` on each option so the branches are real.
#   * Events mutate the Orville systems (fleets, government) as the war turns.

event "Kaylon signal detected"
	date 1 12 3013

mission "Kaylon Signal [0]"
	landing
	non-blocking
	name "Border Observatory"
	description "Union xenolinguists need <cargo> delivered to the border observatory on <destination>. They have detected something out past the line."
	cargo "Union Expedition Supplies" 10 30
	source
		government "Republic"
		attributes "spaceport"
	to offer
		has "event: Kaylon signal detected"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Admiral Halsey's face is on your screen before you have finished docking. "Captain. A Union observatory on <destination> has picked up a repeating transmission. It is not Krill, it is not Moclan, and it is not anything in our database."`
			`She pauses. "We need a xenology team up there with survey equipment, and we need them up there yesterday. Can we count on you?"`
			choice
				`	"I'll take the team and the gear."`
					goto accept_signal
				`	"What exactly are we dealing with?"`
					goto explain
				`	"Not my problem, Admiral."`
					decline
			label explain
			`"A repeating pattern of prime numbers, geometric solids, and what our people believe is a census. Something out there is counting. Something out there wants to be counted."`
			choice
				`	"Understood. I'll take the team."`
					goto accept_signal
				`	"That's not comforting."`
					decline
			label accept_signal
			`The xenolinguists load a crate of <cargo> into your hold within the hour. "Whatever this is," the lead researcher says, strapping in, "we are about to meet it."`
				accept
	on complete
		payment 30000
		set "kaylon arc: prologue"
		log "Delivered a xenology team to the border observatory. The repeating signal was a sequence of prime numbers and geometric solids -- a census, not a greeting."
