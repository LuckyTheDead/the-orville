event "Krill border raid"
	date 12 11 3014
	system "Ekos"
		government "Krill"
		remove fleet "Small Krill Patrol"
		remove fleet "Large Krill Patrol"
		fleet "Krill Raider" 800

mission "Krill border raid"
	landing
	non-blocking
	source
		government "Krill"
		attributes "spaceport"
	to offer
		has "event: Krill border raid"
	on offer
		log "Factions" "Krill" `Intelligence reports a Krill border raid on the Ekos system. The Krill are expanding their temple network aggressively, and the Planetary Union has placed the fleet on high alert.`
		conversation
			`You receive a priority transmission from Admiral Hanson. A Krill task force has attacked the Ekos system. The Ekos colony is a Moclan research outpost -- if the Krill succeed, it will be a major escalation.`
			`	Understood, Admiral. I'm heading to Ekos now. Any additional instructions?`
			`	Maintain defensive posture. If the Krill are driven off, the Moclan Hegemony has offered a research cooperation treaty. If not, prepare for a Krill counterstrike.`
			choice
				`	"I'll engage the Krill task force immediately."`
				`	"What about the Moclan outpost's survival?"`
				`	"I'll notify the Union Science Council first."`

			label krill_engage
			`You're right. The Krill won't stop until they've secured the Mid Rim. I'll engage.`
			`	Scanning the battle map, you see three Krill cruisers engaging the Union patrol near Ekos. The battle is evenly matched, but reinforcements are two hours out.`
			choice
				`		Push the attack -- destroy the Krill formation."`
				`		Break off and retreat to Union lines."`

			label krill_destroy
			`Your fighters score direct hits on all three Krill cruisers. The lead ship explodes in a fireball, and the other two break retreat toward Krill space. The Ekos system is secure, but the victory comes at a cost -- your ship's shields are at 15%.`
			action
				set "krill conflict: engaged"
				set "union standing: improved"
			`Admiral Hanson commends you: Excellent work, Captain. The Krill will think twice before attacking a Union outpost again.`
			goto krill_end

			label krill_retreat
			`You break off the engagement and fall back to Union lines. The Krill cruisers pursue for a moment, then return to their temple network. Admiral Hanson is dissatisfied but understands the strategic reality.`
			action
				set "krill conflict: avoided"
			`The Krill will remember this, Captain. But for now, the Ekos system remains under Union protection.`
			goto krill_end

			label moclan_concern
			`Understood, Admiral. The Moclans have been protective of their research outposts. I'll factor that into my response.`
			goto union_response

			label union_response
			`Transmission ends. You plot a course for Ekos, Union sensors picking up Krill fleet movements. The Mid Rim is about to become very interesting indeed.`
			goto krill_end

			label krill_end
			decline

event "Moclan schism resolution"
	date 5 2 3015
	system "Otranta"
		government "Moclan"
		remove fleet "Small Moclan Science Vessel"
		remove fleet "Large Moclan Science Vessel"
		fleet "Moclan Heavy Cruiser" 500

mission "Moclan schism resolution"
	landing
	non-blocking
	source
		government "Moclan"
		attributes "spaceport"
	to offer
		has "event: Moclan schism resolution"
	on offer
		log "Factions" "Moclan" `A Moclan schism has reached a critical point. A faction of conservative scientists has seceded from the Hegemony, taking several heavy cruisers with them. They're hoarding biogenic weapons research and threatening to use it unless the Hegemony meets their demands. The Planetary Union has been asked to mediate.`
		conversation
			`The Moclan High Council has convened an emergency session. The schism threatens to tear the Hegemony apart, and the biogenic research could change the balance of power in the Mid Rim.`
			`	What does the Union want us to do, Admiral?`
			`	We want you to mediate. The reformists are willing to share their research if the Hegemony guarantees their safety. The conservatives refuse to back down.`
			choice
				`	"Support the reformists -- share the research with the Union."`
				`	"Back the High Council -- suppress the schism by force."`
				`	"Remain neutral -- let the Moclans work it out themselves."`

			label reformist_path
			`You accept the reformists' offer. The biogenic research is shared, and the Hegemony agrees to Union protection in exchange. The schism is resolved without bloodshed, and the Union gains valuable scientific data.`
			action
				set "moclan schism: resolved"
				set "union standing: improved"
			decline

			label council_path
			`You side with the High Council. Moclan forces move against the reformist fleet. The battle over Otranta is brief but bloody -- three heavy cruisers are destroyed before the reformists surrender.`
			action
				set "moclan schism: suppressed"
				set "moclan standing: improved"
			`Admiral Hanson is troubled by the outcome. The biogenic research is lost, but the Hegemony remains intact. The Union watches warily.`
			decline

			label neutral_path
			`You decline to intervene. The Moclans resolve the schism internally, but the biogenic research disappears into the hands of the conservatives. The Union loses a potential ally and a valuable resource.`
			action
				set "moclan schism: unresolved"
			`Admiral Hanson understands but is disappointed. The Mid Rim just got more dangerous.`
			decline

mission "Kaylon incursion"
	landing
	non-blocking
	source
		government "Kaylon"
		attributes "spaceport"
	to offer
		has "event: Kaylon incursion"
	on offer
		log "Factions" "Kaylon" `Kaylon vessels have crossed the Union border near the Kaylon system. Their signature is unmistakable -- synthetic, efficient, and utterly without mercy. The Planetary Union has declared a state of emergency.`
		conversation
			`Urgent priority transmission from Union Command. Kaylon vessels have crossed the border. They're not hailing, not negotiating -- just advancing.`
			`	What are our orders, Admiral?`
			`	Engage at will. The Kaylon must not penetrate Union space. Use maximum force.`
			choice
				`	"Understood. I'll engage the Kaylon vessels."`
				`	"Can we attempt communication first?"`
				`	"Recommend full fleet mobilization before engagement."`

			label kaylon_engage
			`You engage the Kaylon vessels. They fight with ruthless efficiency, adapting to your tactics within minutes. Your ship takes heavy damage, but you drive them back to the border.`
			action
				set "kaylon incursion: repelled"
				set "union standing: improved"
			`Admiral Hanson: Well done, Captain. The Kaylon have been repelled -- for now. But they're learning from every encounter.`
			decline

			label kaylon_comm
			`You attempt to hail the Kaylon lead vessel. No response. They continue advancing, scanning your ship with clinical precision. Without warning, they open fire. You have no choice but to engage.`
			action
				set "kaylon incursion: repelled"
			`The Kaylon are repelled, but their silence speaks volumes. They don't negotiate. They don't surrender. They only compute.`
			decline

			label kaylon_mobilize
			`You recommend full fleet mobilization. Admiral Hanson agrees, but the nearest Union fleet is four hours away. By the time they arrive, the Kaylon will have penetrated deep into Union space.`
			action
				set "kaylon incursion: delayed"
			`You engage the Kaylon alone to buy time. The battle is desperate, but you hold them long enough for the Union fleet to arrive. The Kaylon withdraw, calculating the odds are no longer in their favor.`
			action
				set "union standing: improved"
			`Admiral Hanson: You saved the sector, Captain. The Union owes you one.`
			decline

event "Calivon zoo incident"
	date 11 3 3015
	system "Calivon"
		government "Calivon"
		remove fleet "Small Calivon Ship"
		fleet "Calivon Cruiser" 300

mission "Calivon zoo incident"
	landing
	non-blocking
	source
		government "Calivon"
		attributes "spaceport"
	to offer
		has "event: Calivon zoo incident"
	on offer
		log "Factions" "Calivon" `A Calivon vessel has been detected near Union space. The Calivon consider less advanced species as animals -- suitable for collection and display. A Union freighter has gone missing near Calivon territory.`
		conversation
			`Admiral Hanson's face appears on your screen, pale. "Captain, we've lost contact with the Union science vessel Descartes near Calivon space. The Calivon have claimed it."`
			`	They've taken the crew?`
			`	They consider us animals, Captain. The crew is now exhibits in a Calivon zoo. We need you to retrieve them -- discreetly. The Calivon don't take kindly to rescue missions.`
			choice
				`	"I'll infiltrate Calivon space and rescue the crew."`
				`	"Can we negotiate their release?"`
				`	"Too risky. Recommend abandoning the crew."`

			label calivon_rescue
			`You slip past Calivon sensors using a stealth approach. The Descartes crew is held in a display habitat, observed by Calivon researchers who treat them like curiosities. You extract the crew without detection, but not before the Calivon record your ship's signature.`
			action
				set "calivon incident: rescued"
				set "calivon standing: worsened"
			`Admiral Hanson: The crew is safe, Captain. But the Calivon now know our capabilities. They'll be watching.`
			decline

			label calivon_negotiate
			`You attempt to open diplomatic channels with the Calivon. They respond with amusement. To them, negotiating with "lesser species" is like negotiating with livestock. They offer to trade the crew for Union technology -- specifically, your ship's quantum drive.`
			action
				set "calivon incident: negotiated"
			`You refuse the trade. The Calivon withdraw their offer but keep the crew. A diplomatic incident looms.`
			decline

			label calivon_abandon
			`You recommend abandoning the crew. Admiral Hanson is silent for a long moment. "Understood, Captain. The Descartes crew is officially listed as missing in action."`
			action
				set "calivon incident: abandoned"
			`The Union mourns the lost crew, but Calivon relations remain stable. For now.`
			decline

Though they do not speak, Union xenolinguists have analyzed the patterns and believe it may be an overture for diplomatic contact. 		conversation
			`Union xenolinguists have decoded the Kaylon signal. It's a sequence of prime numbers and geometric shapes -- clearly intentional.`

				`	"Hail them on open frequencies -- attempt simple concepts."`
