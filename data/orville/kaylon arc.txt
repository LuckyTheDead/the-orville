event "kaylon arc 2 begins"
	date 1 1 3013
	set "kaylon arc 2: start"

mission "Kaylon Prologue 0"
	landing
	non-blocking
	name "The Signal: Border Observatory"
	description "A Union observatory has detected a repeating transmission out past the line. Carry the xenology team to <destination>."
	cargo "Kaylon Mission Supplies" 5 20
	deadline
	source
		government "Republic"
		attributes "spaceport"
	to offer
		has "event: kaylon arc 2 begins"
	on offer
		conversation
			`A contact is waiting for you near the landing pads, and they are not the sort of person who waits twice.`
			`They have a job. They have the cargo already crated. What they do not have is a ship they trust.`
			choice
				`\t"I'll see it through."`
					accept
				`\t"What are we up against?"`
					goto explain
				`\t"Not my fight."`
					decline
			label explain
			`A repeating pattern of prime numbers, geometric solids, and what our people believe is a census.`
				accept
	on complete
		payment 40000
		set "kaylon arc 2: ch0"
		log "Delivered a xenology team to the border observatory. The repeating signal was a census, not a greeting."
